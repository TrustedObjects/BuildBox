# Further reading

To keep the usual commands at hand, the [cheat sheet](/cheatsheet.pdf) holds them on a single page, written for people who build and test a project without being developers. Print it and keep it next to you.

To go further, you can read the [user manual](/user/), presenting BuildBox with more details, and also all available user commands to manage your projects.

Later, you can take a look at the [developer manual](/dev/), useful to understand how to develop scripts using BuildBox API, and also to develop BuildBox itself.
